What I Offer

Discreet, Relational & Psychodynamic Therapy| Online Therapy, London

  • I work with a wide range of clients navigating burnout, perfectionism, anxiety, and difficulty in relationships. My particular experience is with people in demanding, high-pressure careers, especially finance, consultancy, and corporate roles, having spent over a decade in these worlds myself.
  • What brings you in is usually only part of it. You might come to therapy because of burnout, stress, or a difficult decision about your career. But sometimes what brings you through the door is only part of the story. The deeper issue might be anxiety, patterns in your relationships, a perfectionism that doesn't let up, or a sense that you're chasing a version of success that no longer fits.
  • My approach is relational and depth-oriented. Together, we explore how past patterns may be shaping your present — in your work, relationships, and sense of self. I’m less interested in quick fixes and more interested in exploring what is really driving the way you move through the world.
  • I draw on psychodynamic, attachment, and CBT based approaches. Psychodynamic work looks at how patterns formed early in life continue to shape you now. Attachment theory explains how those early relationships shaped your capacity for trust and closeness. CBT offers practical tools where a specific pattern of thinking is keeping you stuck. Together, they help make sense of not just what's happening, but why, and what can shift.

About Me

From the City to the Therapy Room

  • Before Therapy Before training as a therapist, I spent over a decade working in finance and consultancy — across London, San Francisco, Australia and France. I know what it means to perform under pressure and what it costs to keep up appearances while privately struggling.
  • My Philosophy The patterns we developed early in life — in our first relationships, our families, the environments we grew up in — don't stay in the past. They show up in how we work, how we love, how we cope, and how we understand ourselves.
  • Outside the Therapy Room You'll usually find me somewhere outside. Walking, hiking, or looking for that feeling of being small in a landscape. I think joy, awe, and genuine connection matter more than we often admit.
